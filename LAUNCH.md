# LAUNCH — the first 30 days (cold-start playbook)

A 0-follower account needs a different playbook than steady state. Both verticals carry
`launch_mode: true` in `config.yaml`; flip to `false` after month one.

## What launch_mode changes
- **Hashtags:** heavier niche-tag mix (the caption engine widens NICHE picks from 6 → 8 and
  drops one broad tag- reach comes from niche discovery at 0 followers, not the broad feed).
- **Cadence:** 1-2/day consistently beats bursts. Generate the night before, approve in the morning.
- **Buffer-first:** hold 5 approved reels before the first post ever goes up.

## The opening 9-grid (the shop window)
Before following anyone, a visitor sees 9 tiles. Make them a coherent statement:
1-3. "Paper vs Reality" tests with the strongest visual gap (equity race + counting stat card)
4-6. one confirm ("this one held up"), one myth-bust, one drawdown-focused reel
7-9. per-lane flavour: Priced In → buy-the-dip / DCA / timing-the-market;
     Overfit → vol surface explainer, Kelly curve, implied-vs-realised
Pull all 9 from the seed bank (SEEDING.md)- they exist before the harvester runs once.

## Launch-month behaviours (manual, 15 min/day)
- Follow + genuinely comment on 5-10 accounts/day in the niche (fintok, quant-adjacent).
- Reply to EVERY comment in the first hour after posting (the algorithm reads reply velocity).
- Add the suggested trending audio in-app at post time (caption.txt carries the pick)-
  trending audio is the single biggest cold-start reach lever.
- Post times: start 7-8am + 6-8pm UK; stage 12 finds the real best times after ~10 posts.

## Graduation criteria (switch launch_mode off when)
- 30 days elapsed AND ≥ 20 posts up AND the buffer holds ≥ 5 approved reels.
- Then the steady pipeline + feedback loop take over.
